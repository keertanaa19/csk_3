Frequently asked questions

What does CSK PF Consulting Services help with?

We help employees with PF claims, transfers, UAN and KYC updates, record corrections and pension questions, and help employers with PF and ESI registration, monthly compliance and DSC approval.

My PF claim was rejected. Can you help?

Yes. We check why the claim was rejected, help correct the details and resubmit it. We can also follow up on claims that are still pending.

Which documents will I need to share?

It depends on the service. Common documents include Aadhaar, PAN, bank details and your UAN for PF services, and business identity and address proof for registration. We will give you a checklist for your case.

How do I get started?

Call us, message us on WhatsApp or send an enquiry through the form. We will listen to your issue and explain what to do next.
